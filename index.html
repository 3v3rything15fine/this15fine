Workshop Overview & Goal

  • Purpose: Collaborative brainstorm to enhance TXST student financial wellness this summer.

Our Ideation Framework: Positive Focus

In the Ideate phase we build on strengths and opportunities. Appreciation Inquiry guides positive inquiry and sustainable solutions.

  • Risk Management: Insurance, emergency funds

  • Employment & Income: Earnings, taxes
